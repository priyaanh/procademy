Privacy

Last updated November 2, 2026

Procademy is a free math practice website. It has no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and nothing about you is sold or shared for marketing. This page says what leaves your device, where it goes, and how to delete it.

Without an account

Everything (your levels, practice history, settings, profile, badges and tutor chats) is saved in this browser, on this device only. Nobody else sees it.

With an account

An account is optional. It keeps the same progress on every computer you use. Accounts run on Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore).

  • What is saved online: your username, when the account was made and last saved, and the same progress kept on the device: levels, practice history, settings, your profile (the name, grade and avatar you chose), badges and speed-sprint bests. Tutor chats and attached files are never uploaded.
  • Who can see it: you, when you are signed in. Each account can read and change only its own record, and nobody can browse the records. The site’s owner, who runs the Firebase project, can also see them.
  • Passwords are kept by Firebase Authentication, scrambled (hashed), and Procademy never sees them. A username account needs no email. An email, if you give one, gets one message with a link to confirm it is yours (the account saves online once it is clicked), and is otherwise used only to reset a forgotten password.
  • Google, GitHub or Facebook: if you sign in with one of these, that service confirms who you are. Firebase Authentication then receives your account’s ID there, and the name, email address and profile picture the service shares. Procademy shows only the email, to you, on your Profile page; it does not save the name or picture with your progress. It never posts anything for you or asks for your friends, contacts or anything else. GitHub and Facebook accounts are only for people aged 13 and over (their rules); anyone can use a username and password instead.

The tutor

When you ask the tutor a question, it is sent to a free AI service, LLM7 or Pollinations, along with the course or skill it is about and your grade, so it can answer at your level. Your name is not sent. If you attach a photo or a PDF, its words are read on your device, and only that text goes with the question. When the tutor draws a picture, a short description of the picture is sent to Pollinations. When you practice a topic you type on the Practice page, the topic and your grade go to the same free AI, which writes the questions. LLM7’s privacy policy says it is not meant for children under 13; it can be switched off in the tutor’s settings. To keep every question on your device, switch the tutor to run on this device instead. Chats are saved on your device only.

Other services

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Some parts are downloaded the first time they are used: the sign-in code from Google, the photo and PDF readers from jsDelivr, and the on-device tutor’s model from Hugging Face. Like any website visit, those servers see your browser’s request (including its IP address). Procademy sends them nothing about you.

Deleting your data

  • Your account: sign in, open Profile (the avatar at the top right), and under Your account choose Delete my account. The account and everything saved online are deleted straight away. If you signed in with Google, GitHub or Facebook, that service asks you to confirm it is you first.
  • This device: log out without keeping a copy, or in Settings choose Erase everything on this device.
  • Facebook: you can also remove Procademy in Facebook’s settings, under Apps and websites. That stops Facebook sign-in; to delete your progress as well, delete the account in Procademy as above.
  • Cannot sign in any more? Open an issue at github.com/priyaanh/procademy/issues (do not write your email address there, because issues are public), and the site’s owner will reply with how to show which account is yours.

Questions and changes

Ask at github.com/priyaanh/procademy/issues. If this page changes, the date at the top changes too, and every earlier version is kept in the site’s history.
